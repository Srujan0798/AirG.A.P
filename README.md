# AI for the Air-Gapped Economy — Six-Deliverable Handoff

**Prepared:** 30 October 2026  
**Source material:** Founder-provided Gemini AI export, YC Winter 2027 RFS image, 30-skill chart and current primary-source research.  
**Purpose:** Transfer the founder's intent and a research/engineering operating system into a separate IDE and advanced-agent environment.

## The six deliverables

### Phase I — Strategic intelligence and handoff

1. [`01_MASTER_VISION_AND_STRATEGY.md`](01_MASTER_VISION_AND_STRATEGY.md) — original intent, opportunity framing, recommended product wedge, market hypotheses, strategic principles and validation gates.
2. [`02_INDEPENDENT_RESEARCH_AGENT_PROTOCOLS.md`](02_INDEPENDENT_RESEARCH_AGENT_PROTOCOLS.md) — master research contract, 12 specialist prompts, 2–3 research-plugin candidates, search map, evidence grading and synthesis protocol.
3. [`03_IDE_AGENT_AND_STARTUP_INITIALIZATION.md`](03_IDE_AGENT_AND_STARTUP_INITIALIZATION.md) — environment separation, repository structure, agent topology, plugin setup controls, memory, startup prompt and checklist.

### Phase II — Product engineering

4. [`04_TRADITIONAL_SYSTEMS_ENGINEERING_BLUEPRINT.md`](04_TRADITIONAL_SYSTEMS_ENGINEERING_BLUEPRINT.md) — product requirements, reference architecture, models, orchestration, memory, sandboxing, security, offline lifecycle and evaluation.
5. [`05_PLUGIN_POWERED_ENGINEERING_FRAMEWORK.md`](05_PLUGIN_POWERED_ENGINEERING_FRAMEWORK.md) — all 30 skills from the supplied chart, likely roles, dispositions, shortlists and security/license evaluation rubric.
6. [`06_CUSTOM_HYBRID_ENGINEERING_METHODOLOGY.md`](06_CUSTOM_HYBRID_ENGINEERING_METHODOLOGY.md) — the final methodology combining traditional engineering, agentic work, plugins, research, custom integration and startup execution.

## Central recommendation

Investigate a **secure offline agentic engineering workbench** as the first product wedge into the wider air-gapped AI economy. Start with repository understanding, requirements traceability, test generation, legacy modernization and reviewed patch proposals in a non-production, isolated engineering environment. Do not begin with agents controlling live industrial systems or with training a new foundation model.

This is a hypothesis to validate, not a proven market fact. The research protocol is intentionally designed to disconfirm it.

## How to use the package

1. Copy this directory into a new Git repository in the target IDE.
2. Read deliverable 1 first and preserve the founder's intent.
3. Give deliverable 2 to the lead research agent; dispatch its specialist prompts as separate workstreams.
4. Set up the repository and agents using deliverable 3.
5. Do not freeze the architecture until research and customer validation produce evidence.
6. Use deliverables 4–6 for the engineering path once a first workflow is selected.
7. Maintain a claim ledger, ADRs, threat model, benchmark tasks and customer evidence in version control.

## Evidence and limitations

- The founder's intentions are taken from the uploaded Gemini export and statements in the conversation. AI-generated claims in that export are not treated as facts.
- The YC opportunity is verified against the official YC Winter 2027 RFS page.
- Technical recommendations are based on public primary sources and current official documentation where available.
- The 30-skill list is based on a founder-provided image snapshot. Some canonical repositories or exact labels require verification before installation. Popularity/stars are not treated as a quality or security signal.
- Customer demand, market size, willingness-to-pay, product moat, hardware budget and regulatory/accreditation path remain hypotheses until validated.
- No certification, compliance, security guarantee or YC selection is promised.
