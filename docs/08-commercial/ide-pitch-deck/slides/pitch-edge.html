We mapped about 38 products and open-source tools across six layers. Every layer below us already has credible players — so we don’t compete with them, we sit on top of them. Tabnine already sells an air-gapped coding assistant with its own access controls and audit logs, but that governance covers only Tabnine’s platform. What nobody ships is a vendor-neutral governing layer: identity, approvals, signed offline updates and audit evidence across whatever models and agents a lab chooses. That is our position.
