We mapped about 38 products and open-source tools across six layers. Every layer below us already has credible players — so we don’t compete with them, we sit on top of them. Tabnine, Codeium, GitLab Duo and Cline Enterprise each sell air-gapped assistants with their own access controls and audit logs, but each governs only its own product. Palantir’s Security Forge proved the model — governed, model-agnostic agents with evidence behind every finding — but it is locked to the Palantir stack, scoped to cyber defence, and sold to US government customers. Nobody ships a vendor-neutral layer that governs competing agents across the non-security engineering workflow — requirements, code, tests, change impact — with signed offline intake and an accreditation evidence pack. That is the layer we own.
