## Route 1 — iDEX DISC / Open Challenge (SPARK grant)

- **What:** Ministry of Defence framework run by Defence Innovation Organisation (DIO) under Dept of Defence Production. Grants via Defence India Start-up Challenges (DISC) and Open Challenges.
- **Ticket:** SPARK grant up to **₹1.5 crore**; iDEX Prime cases up to **₹10 crore**. Milestone-based tranches. May include access to defence testing facilities, mentorship, partner incubators.
- **Eligibility:** DPIIT-recognized startups, MSMEs, individual innovators; academic/research teams may apply through permitted categories; challenge-specific themes apply.
- **Timeline:** Challenge call → screening by high-powered committee → grant agreement → milestone tranches. Exact call cadence not found; check idex.gov.in live challenges.
- **Official source:** DDP MoD iDEX page (https://www.ddpmod.gov.in/en/offerings/schemes-and-services/idex) [A] · iDEX how-to-apply (https://idex.gov.in/how_to_apply) [A] · ISTI portal (https://www.indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in/funding-opportunities/startups/idex-innovations-defence-excellence) [A], accessed 2026-11-11.
- **Fit:** High — direct defence/aerospace software buyer, non-dilutive, validates with MoD.
- **Student-team limits:** Individual innovators must form a startup before signing the grant agreement [A]. Access to defence testing facilities / classified environments likely needs security clearance — a student team would not hold it quickly (D inference, not found in source).

## Route 2 — iDEX Prime

- **What:** Larger-scale iDEX track for higher-innovation, higher-investment cases.
- **Ticket:** Up to **₹10 crore** [A].
- **Eligibility:** Startups/MSMEs; iDEX category.
- **Timeline:** Not found (call-dependent).
- **Official source:** DDP MoD iDEX page [A], accessed 2026-11-11.
- **Fit:** Medium-high — but likely too large/early for a first student pilot; better as follow-on.

## Route 3 — ADITI (iDEX sub-scheme)

- **What:** "Acing Development of Innovative Technologies with iDEX"; critical/strategic defence technologies with export potential; outlay ₹750 crore for 2023-24 to 2025-26; launched 4 Mar 2024.
- **Ticket:** Grant-in-aid up to 50% of Product Development Budget, max **₹25 crore** per project.
- **Eligibility:** Startups/MSMEs/individual innovators; individuals must register as startup/MSME before signing grant agreement.
- **Timeline:** Scheme window ends 2025-26 — near expiry; confirm live calls.
- **Official source:** DDP MoD ADITI details (https://www.ddpmod.gov.in/en/offerings/innovations/aditi_details) [A] · ISTI portal (https://www.indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in/funding-opportunities/startups/aditi-acing-development-innovative-technologies-idex) [A], accessed 2026-11-11.
- **Fit:** Medium — ticket is large and window closing; software/air-gapped AI may not match listed critical-tech focus (not found whether eligible).

## Route 4 — DRDO Technology Development Fund (TDF)

- **What:** MoD programme executed by DRDO for indigenous defence/dual-use tech; industry may collaborate with academia/research institutions.
- **Ticket:** Up to **₹50 crore** per project; up to **90%** of total project cost; max development period **4 years**.
- **Eligibility:** Indian industry, preference to MSMEs and DPIIT-recognized startups.
- **Timeline:** Multi-year; heavy compliance; not suited to a fast first pilot.
- **Official source:** ISTI portal (https://www.indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in/funding-opportunities/startups/drdo-tdf-technology-development-fund) [A] · Gujarat Startup TDF page (https://startup.gujarat.in/schemes/technology-development-fund-tdf-scheme) [C], accessed 2026-11-11.
- **Fit:** Low for first pilot — too large/slow; relevant later for scale-up.

## Route 5 — GeM + Startup India public procurement

- **What:** Government e-Marketplace; DPIIT-recognized startups and MSMEs can register as sellers and sell to ministries/departments. Startup India public-procurement page links GeM Startup Runway.
- **Ticket:** Tender-dependent; not found.
- **Eligibility:** DPIIT-recognized startup / MSME seller registration on GeM.
- **Timeline:** Tender cycle, bid evaluation, PO — typically months; not found for software category.
- **Official source:** Startup India public procurement (https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/public_procurement.html) [A], accessed 2026-11-11.
- **Fit:** Medium — route to defence PSU/ministry buyers; GeM categories may not cleanly cover air-gapped AI software (not found); tender competitiveness high.
- **Gujarat note:** GoG removed "prior experience", "turnover", "tender fee", "EMD" for startups in state public procurement (Industries & Mines Dept resolution 11.4.2018) [A via startupindia.gov.in].

## Route 6 — Gujarat SSIP 2.0 (via GUSEC / institute incubation cell)

- **What:** Govt of Gujarat Education Dept policy funding student PoC/prototype and IP filings.
- **Ticket:** PoC/prototype grant up to **₹2.5 lakhs**; IPR support (patent up to ₹75,000 domestic); incubation/workspace.
- **Eligibility:** Currently enrolled student or recent graduate (≤5 years) from Gujarat; applications routed through/backed by a recognized educational institute or university incubation center (e.g., GUSEC).
- **Timeline:** Policy active Jan 2022 to March 2027; five-step apply → screen → pitch → grant.
- **Official source:** GUSEC SSIP 2.0 (https://gusec.edu.in/ssip-2-0) [A] · SSIP Gujarat technical PMU (https://www.ssipgujarat.in/technical_pmu) [A] · Gujarat University innovation page (https://www.gujaratuniversity.ac.in/innovation) [A], accessed 2026-11-11.
- **Fit:** Highest for a student team — IIT Gandhinagar is Gujarat-based; small non-dilutive ticket to build the first offline prototype/pilot. Confirm IITGN institution-level routing (not found whether IITGN is a nodal institute).

## Route 7 — GUSEC Surge / incubation

- **What:** GUSEC resource-access program; grant resources up to **₹2 lakhs** for MVPs/prototypes; co-working, mentorship, investor connect.
- **Ticket:** Up to ₹2 lakhs (resources).
- **Eligibility:** Early-stage startups; incubated via GUSEC.
- **Official source:** Gujarat University innovation page (https://www.gujaratuniversity.ac.in/innovation) [A], accessed 2026-11-11.
- **Fit:** Medium — complements SSIP 2.0 for MVP build.

## Route 8 — Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS) & NIDHI (via GUSEC)

- **What:** GUSEC is DPIIT-selected to deliver SISFS; NIDHI EIR fellowship (stipend ₹30k/month max); NIDHI SSS seed support (₹25 lakh to ₹1 crore); TIDE 2.0 (MeitY, grant up to ₹10 lakhs).
- **Ticket:** SISFS ticket not found; NIDHI SSS ₹25L–₹1 Cr; TIDE 2.0 up to ₹10L.
- **Eligibility:** DPIIT-recognized startup; incubated at approved incubator.
- **Official source:** GUSEC funding page (https://gusec.edu.in/funding) [A/C], accessed 2026-11-11.
- **Fit:** Medium — requires DPIIT recognition first; seed-stage, not defence-specific.

## Security-clearance / export-control limits a student team would hit

- iDEX/DRDO grant agreements: individual innovators must incorporate as a startup before signing [A]. Student founders may lack incorporable entity and prior performance.
- Access to defence testing facilities and classified lab environments: requires security clearance; not found whether student team can obtain quickly (D inference).
- Export control (SCOMET regime for dual-use software/encryption): not found whether air-gapped AI engineering software is controlled; treat as open risk (D inference). Confirm before discussing model weights or code transfer with defence buyers.

## Recommended sequencing (fastest credible)

1. **Now–Month 1:** Incorporate DPIIT-recognized startup; apply SSIP 2.0 via institute incubation cell (IITGN/Gujarat) for ₹2.5L PoC grant. Build offline read-only prototype on one GPU workstation.
2. **Month 2–3:** Apply iDEX DISC/Open Challenge relevant theme (₹1.5L–1.5Cr SPARK) for defence validation + testing-facility access.
3. **Month 3–6:** Convert one design partner (defence/aerospace supplier lab) into a paid pilot via GeM/direct PO or design-partner letter.
4. **Month 6–12:** If pilot converts, pursue iDEX Prime / ADITI / TDF for scale-up; then GeM repeat sales.
